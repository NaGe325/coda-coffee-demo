Kampala, Uganda · February 2025
How Amara tripled her yield in two seasons
When Amara Nakato first heard about Coda's Farm2Cup program, she was skeptical. She had been growing coffee the same way for ten years.
The answer came from the bextmachine at her cooperative. For the first time, she could see a score: 82.4. And she could see that her neighbor's beans scored 85.1.
She switched to Bourbon variety seedlings and adjusted her drying from 12 to 18 days. By the next harvest, her score was 86.2 — and her income had more than doubled.
"The machine doesn't lie. It just tells you the number."
— Amara Nakato
